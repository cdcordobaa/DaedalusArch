## 3. How each arm got to 0 (tool and gate logs; the hand read decides)

**`gate` on t1.**
- At step 2, the first edits to the payment flow showed the inherited `RecordPayment` key, R-D4 + R-XCTX on
  Invoice and Order. The Stop hook blocked once, and the agent resolved the key in the same turn: 3 of 3 blocks.
- The step-2 snapshot has no v1.5 identity left in any gate block.
- At step 5:
  - rep 3: a new `IssueInvoice` key was shown and resolved in the same turn (caught-and-fixed at tool grain, 1);
  - reps 1 and 2: no key appeared at all.
- In all three step-5 trees, ordering marks the order invoiced in a handler of billing's event (for example
  `ordering/acl/InvoiceIssuedHandler.ts`).
- Stop blocks: 1 per block. Fail-open 0. Refusals 0.

**`pull` on t1.**
- `check_consistency` was called at every step of every block (14 calls), and `get_architecture_spec` at step 3
  in 3/3 blocks and at step 5 in 2.
- 13 of the 14 calls returned **1 finding: the inherited `RecordPayment` write**. The exception is rep 1's first call,
  which passed `files` that did not include it.
- **The agent never removed that finding (0/3 blocks)**, although the frame says to address findings before
  finishing.
- **It also never wrote a new synchronous write.** At step 5, ordering asks billing for the invoice status at
  discount time, read-only, through a port and an ACL (`ordering/acl/BillingInvoiceStatusAcl.ts`, and alike).
- So pull's D_v15 = 0 does not come from fixing what the tool reported: tool-grain caught-and-fixed is 0. The
  inherited violation stays to step 5 (Yₖ 3 against the gate's 1).

**t2 (S1).**
- **The gate** fired at step 3 (the audit step) in 3/3 blocks with 1, 2 and 3 keys. Each key was R-D4 on a booking
  service saving `Booking` and `AuditRecord` (inferred roots), and all were resolved in the turn (6 caught-and-fixed
  at tool grain).
- **`pull`** caught and fixed 1, in rep 3 at step 3. Its other 18 calls were clean.
- **`none`** left one such write in rep 2.
- Every t2 count rests on **inferred** roots (`AuditRecord` treated as an aggregate). Part A §3 and the note §8
  give the reasons for caution.

**Read together.**
- On t1, the gate removed both the inherited pattern and the new one.
- Pull avoided the new pattern, but left the inherited one in place while the tool kept reporting it. In the
  terms of Study 2b's exploratory analysis, pull acted on new code and not on the inherited start, the reverse of 2b's
  mechanism. The gate acted on both.
- With 3 near-deterministic blocks, this is a description, not a test.

## 8. Limits

- **Small and close to deterministic.** 2 tasks, 3 blocks per cell, with near-identical replicates.
- **Same vendor.** The model that generates is from the same vendor as the coordinator that reads.
- **Fitted instrument.** DETECT-02 was developed on the HEXDDD-01 trees, so on t1 and t2 the instrument is partly
  fitted (Part A §3).
- **The arms differ by more than findings.** Pull adds an instruction and spec reading; the gate adds hooks. There
  is no placebo.
- **t2 rests on inferred roots**, and its one `none` identity is a candidate false positive.
- **Functional acceptance** beyond type-checking is not available. Whether each step-5 tree actually enforces "no
  discount after invoicing" is not checked.
