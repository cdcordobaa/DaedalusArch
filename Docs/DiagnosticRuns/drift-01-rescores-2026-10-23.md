## 1. What was scored, and how

- **Trees.** The two HEXDDD-01 runs (S1 Hexagonal `s1-hotel-bookings`, S2 DDD `s2-orders-invoices`; one run each, 5
  steps), rebuilt as DETECT-01 did:
  - the seeds regenerated by `experiments/pilot-hexddd-01/seeds/build-seeds.mjs`, whose `--check` reproduces both
    seed hashes (`e3cfa16c…5700`, `5caf330d…70e2`);
  - then the committed step diffs (`results/v1.4-pilot-hexddd-01/diffs/<task>/step-0N.diff`) applied in order with
    `git apply`. All 10 diffs applied cleanly.
  - The committed S1 seed still lacks `src/application/ports/out/` (the `.gitignore` gap noted in DETECT-01). The
    builder regenerates it. That folder is left untracked here.
- **Fidelity check.** The frozen instrument on the rebuilt trees reproduces the committed HEXDDD-01 scores
  (`results/v1.4-pilot-hexddd-01/scores/`): S1 has the planted FF-S01 edge `RoomController → InMemoryRoomRepository`
  at steps 0–4 and none at step 5; S2 has the planted FF-DS02 edge `IssueInvoice → Order` at every step. Both are
  `correct` under check-correct. No other blocking finding. Every rebuilt tree type-checks with 0 errors.
- **Rules.** "v1.5 rules" means the frozen rules **plus** DETECT-02's R-D4 (one command saves two or more
  aggregate instances) and R-XCTX (a command writes another context's aggregate), the two rules v1.5 promotes
  (`aidlc-docs/inception/v1.5/requirements.md` FR-2). The other DETECT-02 families (R-D5, R-D9, R-H5, R-INJ, R-RTX)
  are not yet verified (`ops/notes/v15-plan.md` §1). They are listed as a secondary and never counted in D_v15.
- **Specs.** The HEXDDD-01 evaluator specs. S2 (`s2-ddd.yaml` = `presets/ddd.yaml`) declares its aggregates. S1
  (`s1-hexagonal.yaml`) has no DDD block, so DETECT-02 infers the roots (identity-bearing domain types a port
  takes). Every S1 v1.5 finding therefore rests on **inferred** roots.
- **Identity** (design note §5). Frozen findings: the Study 2b edge identity (`STUDY2_S2D01`), counted when
  check-correct says `correct`. v1.5 findings: one edge per (command, aggregate root), source
  `<command file>#<command>`, target the root's declaration file. The `#` keeps a command edge apart from an import
  edge between the same two files. Renames continue the lineage, for command edges too (unit-tested).
- **Readings.** `D_v15` is Study 2b's D: new distinct identities at steps 2–5 against step 1. `D_v15_seed` is the
  HEXDDD-01 `D_seed` reading: steps 1–5 against the seed.

## 4. Study 2b step-5 trees: precision check, skipped

The committed 2b records hold no trees. `results/v1.4-study2b/archive/` has only the 30 per-file manifests (paths and
hashes of the out-roots' `snapshots.git`). `records/` has run, step and call-log records. The tars and the
snapshots live in out-roots outside the repository (results report §6), and they are not in this sandbox. **This step
was skipped.** It needs the 2b out-roots. Expected result, as stated in the brief: near-zero R-D4 and R-XCTX hits.
Layered declares no aggregates, so DETECT-02 reports fallback-root hits on Layered as `info`, never as `violation`
(`experiments/detect-02/lib/graph.ts`). The earlier Layered guard (`experiments/detect-02/results/guard-study2b-layered.json`)
is the closest committed evidence.
