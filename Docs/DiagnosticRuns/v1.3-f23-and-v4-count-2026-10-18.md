### 2.3 Estimate of expected blocking findings in the 207-case hold-out

The method takes the hold-out's violation cases per style and multiplies them by a ratio from the development set. There are three ratios:

- **Floor.** Assume each violation case carries one blocking finding, and that the share of advisory-only cases is the same as in the development set (the last row of §2.2).
- **Author ratio.** Development expected blocking findings ÷ violation variants, author view.
- **Blind ratio.** The same, using the blind manifests. This is arguably the closer analogue, because the hold-out manifests were also written from the rule sources without sight of the implementation (`aidlc-docs/audit.md:2139`; `Docs/v1.3/holdout-prespec.md:58`).

| Style | Violation cases | Floor | Author ratio | Blind ratio | Range | Below 35? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 33 | 33 × 0.90 ≈ **30** | 33 × 12/10 ≈ **40** | 33 × 14/10 ≈ **46** | 30–46 | possibly |
| **layered (Study 2)** | 32 | 32 × 9/9 = **32** | 32 × 11/9 ≈ **39** | 32 × 10/9 ≈ **36** | 32–39 | possibly (borderline) |
| NestJS | 34 | 34 × 0.80 ≈ **27** | 34 × 10/10 = **34** | 34 × 13/10 ≈ **44** | 27–44 | possibly |
| hexagonal | 32 | 32 × 9/11 ≈ **26** | 32 × 12/11 ≈ **35** | 32 × 18/11 ≈ **52** | 26–52 | possibly |
| **DDD (Study 2)** | 36 | 36 × 0.80 ≈ **29** | 36 × 9/10 ≈ **32** | 36 × 9/10 ≈ **32** | 29–32 (25 if FF-DS01 is excluded) | **yes, in every scenario** |
| pooled | 167 | — | 167 × 54/50 ≈ 180 | 167 × 64/50 ≈ 214 | — | no |

Notes on the table:

- **DDD.** If FF-DS01 findings were kept out of M1 as a deferred tier (`Docs/v1.3/holdout-prespec.md:59`), DDD would lose roughly one more case in ten, giving about 36 × 7/10 ≈ 25. The decision sheet's final Q15 is "stays blocking" (Notion sheet, group 2 summary). If that is entered in ADR-V13-03 before the freeze, the exclusion does not apply.
- **Uncertainty.** The development set has 9 to 11 violation variants per style, so each ratio is itself uncertain by roughly ±0.2 to ±0.4. The 62 → 207 enlargement also aimed at "every blocking construct", which could raise the blocking share above the development floor (`aidlc-docs/audit.md:2187`).
- **Clustering.** Wilson treats findings as independent. Findings cluster within a case and a project (5 apps; `aidlc-docs/audit.md:2139`), so every interval below is somewhat optimistic.

### 2.4 Can V4 pass, per style?

**Thresholds at these sizes.** Each pair is the hits needed, then the misses allowed.

| n | Point ≥ 0.90 (V4 as written) | Point ≥ 0.90 and Wilson LB ≥ 0.80 (board) | Wilson LB ≥ 0.90 ("Validated") |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 23 (≤ 2) | 24 (≤ 1) | impossible |
| 29 | 27 (≤ 2) | 28 (≤ 1) | impossible |
| 32 | 29 (≤ 3) | 31 (≤ 1) | impossible |
| 36 | 33 (≤ 3) | 34 (≤ 2) | 36 (0 misses) |
| 39 | 36 (≤ 3) | 37 (≤ 2) | 39 (0 misses) |

The general rule for Wilson LB ≥ 0.80 is n ≥ 16 with 0 misses, n ≥ 25 with 1, and n ≥ 33 with 2. For LB ≥ 0.90 it is n ≥ 35, 53 and 69, which matches `Docs/v1.3/holdout-prespec.md:75`.

**Chance of passing, if hold-out recall equals the development blind recall** (binomial, exact):

| Style, n | True recall assumed | P(point ≥ 0.90) | P(board rule) |
|---|---|---|---|
| layered, 32 | 0.70 (dev, N105) | 0.01 | 0.00 |
| layered, 36 | 0.70 | 0.00 | 0.00 |
| layered, 36 | 0.78 (pooled, N105) | 0.03 | 0.01 |
| DDD, 32 | 0.78 (pooled) | 0.06 | 0.00 |
| DDD, 32 | 0.90 | 0.60 | 0.16 |
| DDD, 32 | 0.95 | 0.93 | 0.52 |
| any style, 30–46 | 0.78 | 0.02–0.08 | 0.00–0.02 |

DDD's own development blind recall is 9/9. That figure is too small to use, since its interval is [0.70, 1.00]. So for DDD the pooled 0.78 is the basis, with 0.90 and 0.95 shown as what-ifs.

**Reading.**

- **Layered.** n is about 32–39. That is enough for V4 as written and for the board rule, but only with 0–3 misses. With development blind recall at 0.70, layered fails V4 almost surely and would be "characterised". "Validated" needs n ≥ 35 and zero misses.
- **DDD.** n is about 29–32, below 35 in every scenario, so "Validated" cannot be reached even with a perfect result. V4 as written can pass with ≤ 2–3 misses. The board rule can pass only with ≤ 1 miss, which happens about 16% of the time even if true recall is 0.90.
- **Other styles.** At pooled recall 0.78, every style has under a 10% chance of point recall ≥ 0.90.

The binding constraint is recall, not sample size. Sample size decides only which claim levels are open to a style.

### 2.5 Is a V4 ADR needed? Yes. What it should say

D-V4 (`ops/decisions.md:40`) is triggered: DDD is short of 35 in every estimate, and Layered is borderline. The ADR should be dated and merged before `<FREEZE_SHA>`, with no sealed count read. It should state:

1. **The gate, per Study 2 style (Layered, DDD).** Pass = M1 point recall ≥ 0.90 **and** 95% Wilson lower bound ≥ 0.80. Both are reported. The same applies to M3 at ≥ 0.90 / 0.80, and M7 stays at ≥ 0.95. Any style below that is "characterised" and cannot use the blocking outcome in Study 2. This is the board's recommendation.
2. **Why the bar is 0.80 and not 0.90.** At the expected n (Layered about 32–39, DDD about 29–32), LB ≥ 0.90 is out of reach for DDD even when the result is perfect, and needs zero misses for Layered. LB ≥ 0.80 can be reached at n ≥ 25 with one miss.
3. **That this tightens the current gate.** `Docs/v1.3/study2-readiness-gate.md:113` now leaves the lower bound out of the pass, so adding LB ≥ 0.80 makes V4 stricter. It is declared as a pre-run protocol change, not a relaxation. It should also correct `Docs/v1.3/compendium/open-items.md:19` and `ops/02-defensibility-conditions.md:13`, which say the lower bound already blocks V4.
4. **Minimum n.** Fill `<N_PER_STYLE>` at the freeze from the sealed counts. If a Study 2 style has n < 25, it cannot reach LB ≥ 0.80 with even one miss, and the ADR should say in advance that such a style is reported as "characterised" (or "validated at the point estimate" only), not as validated.
5. **The expected outcome, said in advance.** On development evidence (N105: layered 0.70, pooled 0.78), Layered and DDD are both more likely than not to end "characterised". Then Study 2 has no style that can use the blocking outcome (`Docs/v1.3/study2-outline-v2.md:302`). The ADR should name that route now, as in the decision sheet's DDD reality check ("characterised, not confirmatory"), so it is not read later as a goalpost move.
6. **Clustering.** Report a case-clustered interval beside the finding-level Wilson interval, as for C3 external correctness (`ops/01-instrument-map.md:177`).
