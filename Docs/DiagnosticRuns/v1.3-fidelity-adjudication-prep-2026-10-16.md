## Proposed precision and recall (PROPOSED, NOT ADJUDICATED)

Definitions:

- **Precision**: APG edges whose status is required, or acceptable, divided by all APG edges. In **strict** mode, acceptable edges in the APG count as wrong.
- **Recall**: required edges found in the APG, divided by all required edges.
- An edge that is in the APG and in both references is counted as required. The agreement samples support this: all 171 were confirmed.
- Raw and scoped give the same figures, because every out-of-scope edge is acceptable.

**Finding: one gap.** A type-position `import('x').T` (an ImportTypeNode) is neither a statement nor a call, so the extractor never models it. tsc records it.

- It is a real type-only dependency. A layer rule could miss a domain file that names an infrastructure type this way.
- It is recorded as a known gap and pinned by the test. It is not fixed here.
- Neither fidelity corpus contains the construct. The 2026-10-14 harness counts `import('x').T` occurrences, and none disagreed.

Also documented by the fixture:

- In v1.2E, the unresolvable literal counts as `unsupportedDynamic`. In v1.3 it instead warns `EXTRACTOR_002` and is not counted.
- Non-literal specifiers stay unmodelled in all three extractors. For `../plugins/${name}` the possible targets are `src/plugins/*`, which nothing records.
