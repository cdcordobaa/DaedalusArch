# CHECK-3 bases: clean layered controls, regenerated items and static self-check, 2026-10-16

> **No judge or LLM call was made.** This is data construction only. The only model involved was the `mock` provider (prompt harvest). The run itself is pre-registered in `Docs/v1.3/judge-check3-plan.md`, committed before any CHECK-3 call.

## What was wrong in CHECK-2

PR #191 (`v1.3-judge-v22-check2-2026-10-16.md` §0, §3) found the defect. Every CHECK-2 layered base had one business service that held `PrismaService` and called the ORM itself. It was there so that `AP-REPO-BYPASS` had a site.

As a result, every clean FF-N01 business unit was a bypass already, and all three raters failed it 6/6:
- D1 was unreachable;
- REPO-BYPASS paired catch was 3/10;
- CHECK-2 carried no usable FF-N01 evidence.
