Found by the adversarial review of 2026-10-15 (finding B2).

**Method.** `node scripts/v1.3/ffp06-na-recount.mjs` (repository root). It reads only the committed `scores.json` and `evidence/` of each run; nothing was re-run or re-scored, and the tables below are left as they were written. For each style it checks the half of every FF-P06 evidence row (`discriminator[2]`: `FLOWS_TO` or `CONSTRUCTOR_INJECTS`), checks that the blocking per-rule counts sum to the blocking totals, and moves the FF-P06 counts of the styles whose FF-P06 rows are all FLOWS_TO into an N/A bucket. Intervals are 95% Wilson, as in `scripts/v1.3/ue-score.ts` (z = 1.959964).

**N/A bucket (recall unestimated):** 3 FF-P06 FLOWS_TO rows, one each in clean, nestjs and hexagonal, in every run (baseline, after-fixes, this run). In the author and blind-expanded views they were TPs; in the blind-primary view they were FPs.
