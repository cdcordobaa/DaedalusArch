## 2. Runs and reproducibility

- **Lock.** Both live runs held the Neo4j lock for the whole run, in mode `owned`. The `firewall evaluate` children were re-entrant.
- **Determinism.**
  - `run1` (17:22:42Z to 17:24:19Z) and `run2` (17:24:27Z to 17:25:57Z) produced **byte-identical** `raw/` (71 files), `report.md` and `report.json`.
  - The evidence violations are identical for 71/71 cases; only `elapsedMs` differs.
  - Scored the same way, `scores.json`, `scores.md`, `adjudication.csv` and `graph-agreement.json` are byte-identical.
  - `validate-reference.ts --from-raw raw/` reproduces `report.md`, apart from the header line.
- **Admission bypass.** The heavy-command hook blocked on swap (12.1 of 16 GB) while `memory_pressure` reported 34 to 40% free. These commands were each run with `HEAVY_ADMISSION_BYPASS=1`, one at a time: `npm ci`, `tsc`, the jest runs, the hash runs, both live runs and `ue-graph`.
- **Blind manifests.** They were read only through `scripts/v1.3/ue-score.ts --blind`. Nothing else under `daedalus-sealed/` was opened.
