# v1.3 reproducibility audit (2026-10-16)

This is an independent audit of the v1.3 development write-ups dated 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-16. Every headline
number was re-derived from the committed raw outputs (`results/`, `Docs/DiagnosticRuns/<name>/`) without re-running
the tool against Neo4j and without calling any model. Judge results were replayed from the committed cassettes.

- **Branch:** `v1.3-replication`, from `origin/v1.3` at `96a5e77d`.
- **Original docs:** not edited. Every mismatch is recorded here.
- **Replication package:** [`replication/README.md`](../../replication/README.md). It has the environment, setup, the
  per-result commands, and the smoke script that regenerates the offline deterministic results byte for byte.
- **Per-doc tables** (one row per headline number: claimed, recomputed, match, source file) are in the appendices
  [`v1.3-repro-audit-2026-10-16/group-A.md` … `group-E.md`](v1.3-repro-audit-2026-10-16/). Each appendix also
  gives the raw sources used, the regeneration commands tried and a personal-path / secret scan of the files read.

## 1. Verdict

- **No headline result is overturned.** All 15 docs reproduce from the committed raw outputs. No metric, interval
  or count reported as a result is wrong because of a scorer bug, and no scorer bug was found.
- **29 findings were recorded (§2).** Four (C-4, E-4, E-6, E-9) are claims that cannot be checked from committed files. The rest are doc typos and miscounts in prose, wording or definition ambiguities, stale
  numbers, and one qualitative misreading of the judge output (D-3). The two with medium severity are:
  - **C-1:** `apg2-ablation-2026-10-15.md` is stale at the v1.3 head. Its committed outputs reproduce byte for byte
    only at the commit that produced them. At HEAD, cases CP4 and CP5 fail by design after the scope change, and
    nothing in the doc points to that change.
  - **D-3:** the judge-catch docs say the judge "did not mention" the constructs in the missed seeds. The cassettes
    show the opposite: the judge names them and rules them out of the rubric's scope.
- **Some claims cannot be checked from what is committed (§3).** The main ones:
  - inputs that live outside the repository: the 72 E1 outcome projects, the SO4 and corpus checkouts, and the
    sealed blind reference manifests;
  - second-run outputs behind several "byte-identical rerun" statements;
  - test, tsc and hash logs.
- **No secrets are committed.** About 99 committed v1.3 result files record the author's absolute local paths (§4).

## 2. Findings register

Classes:

- **doc typo:** the prose disagrees with the doc's own data.
- **stale number:** true when written, no longer true at the v1.3 head.
- **scorer bug:** the committed aggregate disagrees with its raw rows. **None found.**
- **missing raw file:** the input or the evidence is not committed.
- **ambiguity:** the number is right under one definition, and the doc does not say which.
- **misreading:** the prose interprets the raw output wrongly.

`results/v1.3-labelling/cassettes-*`. The patterns covered:

- Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, GitHub, Slack and AWS key formats;
- private-key headers and bearer tokens;
- `api_key` / `token` / `password` / `secret` assignments;
- `NEO4J_*` values and `bolt://` URIs;
- absolute paths under `/Users`, `/home`, `/Volumes`, `/private/tmp` and `/var/folders`.

- **Secrets: none.** The only `password = '...'` hits are fixture source code inside label plans and cassettes
  (`Password123!` in a corpus project). The `NEO4J_PASSWORD` occurrences in `results/pre-tag/*.json` are already
  `[REDACTED]`. The only `sk-` hits are substrings of "task-management".
