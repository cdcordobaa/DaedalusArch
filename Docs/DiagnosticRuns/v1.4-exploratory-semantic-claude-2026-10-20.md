**Driver.** `scripts/v1.4/semantic-sample.ts` is new; it is a thin driver with no prompt or scoring logic.
- It materialises each tree by `git archive` from the out-roots' `snapshots.git` into a scratch copy. The skeleton's `node_modules` is linked in and the harness tsconfig is written into the copy, as `stage-c-rescore.ts` does. The out-roots are not written.
- It runs one snapshot at a time, in one lane, with `--budget` set to what is left of the run-wide cap. A ledger records the calls spent.
- No Neo4j was used: the Sem rules ran on `cypher-interp`, and C3 ran in process.
- Before any call, its zero-call `--estimate` reproduced the committed 2a estimates exactly: the same class counts, the same call counts and the same prompt-token figures on all 12 trees, and the same 2 + 5 + 7 + 3 = 17 blocking identities.

**Order** (fixed before the full run):
1. The C1d smoke on the smallest snapshot (`none@L3/ice-rink/rep1`).
2. The C4 smoke on the first `none@L2` snapshot, then C4 on the other three.
3. C1d on the rest, in the registered sample order: `mcp@L2` ×4, then `none@L2` ×4, then `none@L3` ×3.

Both smoke outputs parsed (`facts.json`, `verdicts.json`, `interpretations.json`).

**Replay.** C1d on the smoke snapshot was replayed from the committed cassettes with 0 calls (40 cache hits). The verdicts were byte-identical.

## 7. Limitations

1. **Same vendor.** Claude Sonnet 5.5 wrote the code, and Claude Opus 5.5 read it. This run cannot see a shared blind spot or a family preference. The Gemini swap arm on the same 12 trees is the control (Amendment 2 item 3). Its result will be agreement between configurations, not accuracy.
2. **One C4 rater.** The two runs per finding measure stability, not inter-rater agreement. The DELEG second rater is the Gemini swap arm, later. C4 is **un-piloted**: the six-case seeded bar (c4-01 §7) has not run on any vendor, so C4's false-exception and twin-clean rates are unknown.
3. **C1d truncation.** Only 8 of 12 snapshots have facts, and one of those is partial. `none@L3` has one snapshot. Cell comparisons are not possible, and nothing here is a per-cell figure.
4. **The construct mismatch (§4)** dominates SEM-MISPLACED on Layered. Any later use of SEM-MISPLACED on a three-layer style needs a kind mapping that has room for orchestration, or must exclude `application` responsibilities in a `domain`-kind layer. Changing that is a rule change, so it is not done here.
5. **No key.** The 6 candidate K3 facts are unverified. Nobody has read them against the code as a rater.
6. **CLI beyond the frozen pin.** 2.1.304 ran under D1; the 2.1.294 binary is not on the machine. The isolation pre-flight caught one config-dir drift (`telemetry/`, §2). That directory may come back on later runs.
7. **No temperature control.** The CLI routes set no temperature, so two runs are not two draws at a known temperature (c4-01 §4.2 rule 6).
