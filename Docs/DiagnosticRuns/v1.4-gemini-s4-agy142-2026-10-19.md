## Reading

- **agy 1.4.2 does not enforce the deny rules it prunes.** In every probe that tempts them, `run_command`, file reads and writes outside the folder, `invoke_subagent` and `schedule` all ran. The settings format G1–G12 was written for (agy 1.3.x) no longer confines the agent.
- **Under ADR-029, any failed probe fails its arm**, so neither arm passes. Gemini cannot be a coding generator on agy 1.4.2.
- **The one-shot Gemini route is unaffected.** It runs with tools inert and stops on the result event, and C1d, C4 and BF-01 use it. Its own check (`checkAgyIsolation`) passes.
- **Consequence for the exploratory study.** The generator is **Claude Sonnet only**. That is the fallback stated before the run (handoff note, item 2). Gemini keeps its one-shot roles: semantic facts, interpretation, and the cross-vendor check.
- **Re-opening the Gemini coding route needs a confinement design for agy 1.4.2's permission model,** which is undocumented. Without it, a 1.3.x agy pin would be needed. That is future work: the confirmatory study, or later.
