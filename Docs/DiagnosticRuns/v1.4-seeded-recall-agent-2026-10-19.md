# Seeded recall on agent snapshots, and L1 coverage of generated code (2026-10-19)

**Development data only. Not a registered SO4 estimate, no pre-registration.** BUILD-L, unit U10. Zero model calls, no
Neo4j (engine `cypher-interp`, in process), no sealed path read. It answers REV-05 §5 items 1 and 2 with the tooling,
on the agent code already committed; the exploratory study re-runs both on its own snapshots.

## What ran

| Store | Generator | Trajectories (final snapshot) | Snapshots (coverage) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `results/v1.3-floor-probe` | Opus, one session by hand | 12 (6 tasks × `none`, `tool`) | 56 |
| `results/v1.3-floor-probe-2` | Opus, one session by hand | 6 (2 tasks × C1–C3) | 24 |
| `experiments/loop-pilot/run-2026-10-16/outputs` | `claude-sonnet-5-5` in the loop harness | 6 (3 tasks × `none`, `gate`) | 24 |

- **Coverage** (`scripts/v1.4/snapshot-coverage.ts`): every snapshot. Output `results/v1.4-snapshot-coverage/`.
- **Seeded recall** (`scripts/v1.4/seeded-recall-agent.ts`): the final snapshot of each trajectory. Each store ran in
  its own process; `--merge` joined them. Output `results/v1.4-seeded-recall-agent/`.
  - Per operator and snapshot: a seeded sample (seed 20261019) of **at most 2 positive sites and 1 twin site**. That
    cap keeps the run under an hour on 4 cores.
  - The operators are the existing ones: the U5a catalogue operators with symbolic coverage (MO-S01, S03, P01, C04,
    SO01, SO02, CV02, DF01 and their twins) on every style, and the hexagonal/DDD operators on their styles. MO-X01..X03
    (outside coverage) and the judge probes are left out.
  - Type check: the pinned generator skeleton (`scripts/generator/skeleton`, installed with `npm ci --ignore-scripts`
    from its lockfile) under the harness tsconfig. A mutant with more tsc errors than its unmutated snapshot is not
    evaluated.
  - Detection rule: the hex/DDD run's rule (`operators-hexddd.ts`). A positive is detected when every expected key is
    matched by a violation the unmutated snapshot lacks. A twin is a false fire when it adds a blocking violation.

## Seeded recall (positives with an expected key, final snapshots)

| Style | Snapshots | Detected / evaluated | Rate [95 % Wilson] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clean | 5 | 48 / 57 | 0.84 [0.73, 0.91] |
| Hexagonal | 11 | 194 / 216 | 0.90 [0.85, 0.93] |
| Layered, all operators | 6 | 49 / 69 | 0.71 [0.59, 0.80] |
| Layered, without the direction-mismatched operators | 6 | 49 / 49 | 1.00 [0.93, 1.00] |
| DDD | 0 | — | not run (below) |

**Blocking-tier operators.** Every evaluated positive was detected for:
- MO-P01 on all three styles;
- MO-H01, H02, H04, H05, H06 and H07 (hexagonal);
- MO-S03 (layered);
- MO-S01 and MO-DF01 (Clean).

No twin of a matched operator false-fired.

**Where the misses are:**
- **MO-CV02: 0 / 7 (Clean), 0 / 19 (hexagonal).** Its key is an advisory metric. This matches the hex/DDD run's
  reading of CV02 as a threshold rule.
- **MO-SO01: 8 / 10 (Clean), 19 / 22 (hexagonal).** It is an advisory proxy.

**A construct finding, not an instrument miss (Layered).** The U5a catalogue was written against the Clean layer kinds,
where a domain import into infrastructure is the violation.
- `presets/layered.yaml` maps `business` to the domain kind and `persistence` to the infrastructure kind, and closed
  layering allows exactly that step down.
- So under Layered, the MO-S01 and MO-DF01 "positives" add a **legal** edge: 0 / 12 and 0 / 8 detected, correctly.
- Their twins add the **reverse, illegal** edge, and the instrument flags it: MO-S01n 6 / 6, MO-DF01n 2 / 2 blocking
  "false fires" (FF-S01, FF-S02, FF-S03 on the new edge).
- These four operators are marked `direction mismatch` in the tables (`DIRECTION_MISMATCH`), and the Layered total is
  given both ways. A Layered-direction operator is the fix; none was written here.

**No key.** On hexagonal, MO-S01 and MO-S03 had no expected key: 20 rows each. The hexagonal spec does not enable the
Clean templates they target. They are not counted as misses.

## Limits

- **All generators are Claude.** Probes 1–2 are Opus by hand, the pilot is Sonnet. Nothing here measures Gemini code
  shapes; that is what the exploratory study adds.
- **Small n.** Two positive sites per operator per snapshot, final step only, 24 trajectories. The intervals are wide
  per operator.
- **DDD is not covered.** The two pilot DDD snapshots (relief-volunteers) were excluded. Their ISE-frozen spec does not
  compile in the mutation compiler (`loadCompiledSpec`: BR-SPEC-10, FF-DT01/DT02 with empty `aggregates`), although
  the pipeline evaluated them in the pilot with its `emptyAggregates` option. Fixing that needs either a declared
  aggregates spec or the option in the compiler. Not done here.
- **One base excluded from comparison only.** The floor-probe-2 shelter-adoptions C2 snapshot type-checks with 18
  errors unmutated. Its mutants were evaluated when they added no error.
- **Seeds and operators are the existing development operators.** They were written by the same author and sessions as
  the rules. This is a check that the instrument sees seeded defining violations in agent-shaped code, not an
  independent recall estimate.
