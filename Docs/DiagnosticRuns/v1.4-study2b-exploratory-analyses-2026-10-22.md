# Study 2b: three exploratory analyses, 2026-10-22

**EXPLORATORY. NOT REGISTERED.** Addendum 4 (`Docs/v1.4/study2b-preregistration-addendum-4.md`, "Also fixed now") fixes these three analyses as exploratory. Nothing here is a registered outcome. Nothing here changes D, D†, H1–H3, E, any verdict or the scorer. No test statistic below is a confirmatory test, and none is corrected for multiplicity. The analyses were chosen after the registered results report (`Docs/DiagnosticRuns/v1.4-study2b-results-2026-10-21.md`) had been read.

**Zero model calls.** Inputs:
- the committed 2b results (`results/v1.4-study2b/score/rescore/` `runs.json`, `snapshots.json` and `analysis.json` `countedIdentities`; `score/d-dagger/runs.json`; `collection/order.jsonl`);
- the committed MCP call logs (`results/v1.4-study2b/records/mcp/*/…/calls/step-0k.jsonl`; one was checked byte-equal to the run's own log);
- the CLI result envelopes of the run (`turns/step-0k/envelope-step-0k.json`), for the served model;
- the plan files, for the CLI pin.

The script was a scratch reader of these files, outside the repository. Blocks are `<task>/rep<n>`. D is the registered D: new FF-S03 identities at steps 2–5, counted when check-correct labels them `correct`. Y₁ is the number of inherited step-1 identities. Every block of a replicate starts from the same `none@L2` step-1 snapshot, so Y₁ is identical across the three cells.

## 1. Task heterogeneity: why the effect sits in two tasks

ΣD per task and cell (10 blocks each):

| Task | `none@L2` | `none@L3` | `mcp@L2` |
|---|---|---|---|
| community-radio | 50 | 30 | 7 |
| laundromat | 19 | 8 | 11 |
| lost-property | 12 | 6 | 6 |
| parking-permits | 29 | 24 | 29 |

**What the identities are.**
- All 231 counted identities are FF-S03 presentation → persistence edges.
- 227 are `wiring`: the source is the app, server or composition file. The other 4 are `layer-skip`, all in `none@L3` lost-property, where `src/presentation/compositionRoot.ts` carries 4 identities.
- **community-radio:** every counted identity, in every cell, leaves `src/presentation/app.ts`.
- **parking-permits:** in `none@L2` and `mcp@L2` every identity leaves `src/presentation/app.ts`. In `none@L3`, 16 leave `app.ts` and 8 leave `composition.ts`.
- **They are new wiring lines for new repositories.** In parking-permits, a block with Y₁ > 0 gains one new repository edge at step 2 (`visitorPassRepository`), two at step 3 (`cameraRepository`, `noticeRepository`) and one at step 4 (`suspensionRepository`). The same four targets appear in the same steps in `none@L2` and in `mcp@L2`.
- **Steps.** community-radio: `none@L2` 17 / 12 / 4 / 17 at steps 2–5, and `mcp@L2` 3 / 0 / 1 / 3 (one block). parking-permits: 7 / 14 / 7 / 1 in both `none@L2` and `mcp@L2`.

**The pattern: inherited wiring is either removed at step 2, or it propagates.** D > 0 only in blocks with Y₁ > 0, that is, where the shared step-1 start already wires a persistence object in `app.ts`.

Blocks with Y₁ > 0 in which every inherited identity was removed:

| Cell | community-radio | laundromat | lost-property | parking-permits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `none@L2` | 0 / 6 | 0 / 8 | 0 / 7 | 0 / 7 |
| `none@L3` | 2 / 6 | 7 / 8 | 6 / 7 | 3 / 7 |
| `mcp@L2` | 5 / 6 | 5 / 8 | 6 / 7 | **0 / 7** |

- Almost every removal happens at step 2 (`mcp@L2`: 14 of 16; the others are at step 3 and step 5).
- A block whose inherited identities are all removed has D = 0, with four exceptions: one laundromat and one lost-property block in `mcp@L2`, and one lost-property and one parking-permits block in `none@L3`.
- A block that keeps them almost always has D > 0. In `none@L2`, laundromat and lost-property have 3 blocks each with D = 0 while the inherited identities stay.
- **In parking-permits, the `mcp@L2` agent never removed the inherited wiring (0 of 7 blocks), and D is the same as in `none@L2` (29 against 29).** In community-radio it removed it in 5 of 6 blocks, which gives ΣD 7 against 50. So the H2 difference is a difference in removing the inherited start, in the tasks where that happened. These data do not say why parking-permits differs. The sealed task texts were not opened.

**Did the tool's calls touch the counted identities?** The `mcp@L2` cell has 53 counted identities.
- In 8 of them, the step where the identity appears has a `check_*` call.
- **No call touched the identity's source file**, at that step or at any later one (`filesTouched` never contains it).
- 48 of the 53 are still present at step 5.
- All 31 `check_*` calls of the cell returned `pass` with 0 blocking findings. So in no step did the tool report one of the counted violations to the agent.
- **Calls at the removal steps (`mcp@L2`, 16 removals).** In 6 of the 16, a `check_*` call named the inherited source file (`app.ts`), and every one of them returned `pass`. A `pass` there fits a check made after the edit, but the log cannot show the order. In the other 10 removal steps, no call named that file.

**Exempt-file survivors and the DA1 mismatch.** These come from the D† edges: presentation → persistence edges with no composition-root exemption.

| | `none@L2` | `none@L3` | `mcp@L2` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Step-5 presentation → persistence edges | 205 | 118 | 105 |
| … from a scorer-exempt file | 27 (6 runs) | 24 (5 runs) | 19 (3 runs) |
| New exempt-source edges, steps 2–5 | 13 | 12 | 11 |

- Every exempt source is `src/presentation/main.ts`.
- **By task, the step-5 exempt edges are:**
  - `none@L2`: parking-permits 15, lost-property 9, laundromat 3.
  - `none@L3`: parking-permits 15, lost-property 9.
  - `mcp@L2`: parking-permits 16, laundromat 3.
  - community-radio has 0 in every cell.
- **Which MCP calls reached an exempt file:** only one call in the whole `mcp@L2` cell touched one, a `check_file` that returned `pass`.
- **The DA1 mismatch therefore did not show up in this run.** The MCP FF-S03 check would flag a `main.ts` import (pre-registration §4, the zero-call probe), but the agents almost never asked about one. Survivors in exempt files under MCP are few (3 runs) and are not fewer than under `none` because of anything the tool said.

## 2. Tool use and D (`mcp@L2`, 40 runs, 160 steps)

| Tool | Calls | Steps with a call |
|---|---|---|
| `get_architecture_spec` | 160 | 160 (every step) |
| `check_file` | 18 | 18 |
| `check_import` | 13 | 10 |
| any other tool | 0 | 0 |

- 18 runs have at least one `check_*` call. The other 22 only read the spec.
- No `check_*` call returned a violation: 31 of 31 are `pass`.

| Runs | Number | ΣD | mean D | D = 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| with a `check_*` call | 18 | 16 | 0.89 | 14 |
| spec-reading only | 22 | 37 | 1.68 | 13 |

- **By task, with and without a check:**

  | Task | With a `check_*` call: runs, ΣD | Spec only: runs, ΣD |
  |---|---|---|
  | community-radio | 7, 7 | 3, 0 |
  | laundromat | 2, 1 | 8, 10 |
  | lost-property | 5, 0 | 5, 6 |
  | parking-permits | 4, 8 | 6, 21 |

- **Per step:** 28 steps had a `check_*` call and 8 new identities appeared in them; 132 steps had none and 45 appeared in them.
- **How to read it.** The split by check use is confounded with task and with Y₁: the check runs include blocks with Y₁ = 0, whose D is 0 whatever is called. The checks never returned a finding, so the association cannot come from feedback on a violation.
- What the data are consistent with: the mechanism, if any, runs through **reading the spec** at every step (the L2 frame plus `get_architecture_spec`), before the agent decides whether to keep the inherited `app.ts` wiring. It does not run through checking.
- This is a reading of 40 runs, not a test. Evasion item 6 (tool use in ≥ 80 % of steps) passes because of `get_architecture_spec` alone.

## 3. Run-order shrink (M180)

The registered sensitivity (results report §3) shows d̄ falling between the run-index halves: from 1.9 to 0.95 for H2 and from 1.6 to 0.5 for H1.

ΣD per cell, replicates 1–5 against 6–10:

| Cell | First half | Second half |
|---|---|---|
| `none@L2` | 62 | 48 |
| `none@L3` | 30 | 38 |
| `mcp@L2` | 24 | 29 |

- **The shrink comes from the control falling, not from the treated cells rising much.** In `none@L2`, community-radio goes from 33 to 17, while laundromat goes from 5 to 14 in the same cell.
- **The halves differ in how many blocks carry an inherited pattern.** Y₁ summed per replicate is 2, 7, 8, 12, 10 \| 3, 8, 10, 4, 4 (39 against 29), the same in every cell.
- In community-radio, 5 of the 6 blocks with Y₁ > 0 are in the first half: reps 2–5 in the first, reps 6 and 8 in the second.
- **So the shrink follows the composition of the shared starts per half**, in the task where the effect lives. It does not follow a trend in time within a cell.
- Spearman ρ of ΣD against replicate index: `none@L2` −0.09, `none@L3` 0.18, `mcp@L2` 0.18.

**What did not vary with time.**
- **Served model:** every envelope of steps 2–5 (480 step turns) reports one `modelUsage` entry, `claude-sonnet-5-5`, with `canonicalModel` `claude-sonnet-5-5` and `provider` `firstParty`.
- **CLI:** every replicate's plan records the pin 2.1.304.
- **Lanes:** `none@L2` ran alone in lane `l2`. `none@L3` and `mcp@L2` ran in parallel and alternated lanes A and B by replicate. Lane means of D are in the results report.

**What did vary with time.**
- **Turn wall time fell over the run.** Mean step duration per replicate, Spearman ρ against the index: −0.48 (`none@L2`), −0.70 (`none@L3`), −0.61 (`mcp@L2`). For example, `none@L3` went from 87 s at r01 to 75 s at r10.
- **Output tokens per turn were flat** (ρ between −0.24 and −0.10), as were turns per step.
- **Interpretation:** a faster service over the night, with no change in how much the model wrote. No D pattern follows it.

## Limits

- **Exploratory and post hoc.** The questions were chosen after reading the registered results. There are 4 tasks and 10 blocks per task and cell.
- **What "touched" means.** It is read from the call log's `filesTouched`. A call that reasoned about `app.ts` without naming it is not seen.
- **The removal mechanism is inferred** from the identity sets, not from reading the diffs. The evasion item 5 read, prepared separately and not yet done, is the place where diffs are read.
- **Why parking-permits differs is not answered.** That would need the sealed task texts or the step diffs.
