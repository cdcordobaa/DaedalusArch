## 5. Reading and limits

- The question the final study's Hexagonal null left open is answered: with the delivery carrying every scored
  Hexagonal rule, the counted Hexagonal findings fall to zero in every `combined` cell, and the trap rate falls from
  0.79 to 0.11 with acceptance unchanged.
- Population: tasks chosen because the model alone falls into their traps. The result speaks to such tasks, not to
  Hexagonal work at large.
- The outcome D is computed by the same rules the hub delivers, so D = 0 under `combined` shows the agent acts on the
  findings it is shown; the trap rate, checked by each task's own trap check, is the outcome not tied to the delivery.
- The semantic layer is not tested here (DEV-HX-1): the `combined` arm received no R-MIRROR-SEM findings.
- Power was planned at 0.765 for RR 0.25; the observed effect is far larger, so this limit did not bind.
- Cost: a `combined` turn takes about 10 s longer at the median.
- Open: the 8-block hand read (§4) and secondary 7.
