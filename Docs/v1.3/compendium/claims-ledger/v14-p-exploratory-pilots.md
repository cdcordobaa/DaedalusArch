All five are **exploratory tracks merged as exploratory**, on development material: public dev puzzles, fixtures written alongside the detectors, and two small brownfield seeds. None is part of Study 2b, none touches the frozen instrument, and no row here is a held-out or confirmatory figure. **Scope caveats.** n is small everywhere (HEXDDD-01: 2 runs; DETECT-01: 9 cases, 11 pairs, 3 controls; baseline: 5 runs per puzzle); one vendor (Claude Sonnet as generator, Claude sessions as authors of puzzles, oracles, fixtures and hand reads); the hand reads are by a coordinating Claude session with an author spot-check of 2 rows (HEXDDD-01) or pending (baseline). The call ledgers `run/calls.jsonl` of HEXDDD-01 and of the baseline are cited by their reports but not committed (`.gitignore` excludes `*.jsonl`), so those call counts are `exploratory` and rest on the reports and the step records. The PUZZLE-03 baseline has a plan only; no result is committed. `established` here means a mechanical or repository fact (verification verdicts, hashes, file counts, Wilson arithmetic, recorded call counts) confirmed in a committed file, within the dev scope. The earlier secondary rows on HEXDDD-01, DETECT-01 and PUZZLE-01 in part `v14-n` (C8308, C8328–C8357) were checked against these primaries; their figures agree.
