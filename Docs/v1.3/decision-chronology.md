# v1.3 decision chronology

**2026-10-16. Documentation only.** This page dates the v1.3 decisions that a reviewer is likely to ask about. For each one it lists the measurements that already existed when the decision was committed, and whether a human made it or an AI session made it under delegation.

**Method.**
- Dates are commit times from `git log` on `origin/v1.3` (and `origin/v1.2e` where the commit lives only there), in the committer's time zone as recorded. UTC is added where the calendar date differs.
- "Measurements that existed" means result documents committed before the decision commit. Nothing was re-run for this page.
- **Human** means the repository records the author's own words, for example "yes A". **Delegated** means an AI session decided under the author's standing delegation ("max progress", 2026-10-14, or the v1.2E "standing approval"), with no author statement recorded. **Not recorded** means the repository does not say which.
- A decision that came after the data it bears on is marked **after data**.

## Where a decision came after the data

- **Rule tiers (2)** came after Study 1. The protocol's earlier wording, "committed before any labelling", was wrong: the Study 1 labels, P4 included, already existed. The correct statement is "before any v1.3 labelling, precision measurement or tuning" (`Docs/v1.3/rule-tiers.md:3`). It is now corrected in `Docs/v1.3/validation-protocol.md` §5.
- **The hold-out (4, 6)** was sealed after the development baseline. It was enlarged after the after-fixes run and the construct questions, by an agent that could see the development results. The hold-out is therefore not independent of the development data. `Docs/v1.3/holdout-prespec.md` "Known limitations" and `Docs/v1.3/threats-register.md` (enlargement entry) say the same.
- **Construct decisions (5)** are post-data by construction. Their recommendations changed in revision 1 with the figures in view. None of them has been answered.
- **The judge v2.2 rule (8)** was fixed before its own run, but after the v1 and v2 numbers it is compared with.
- **ADR-V13-01 (3)** is the one decision here that a human took before the reference data it affects. It still followed the fidelity measurement that exposed the defect.
