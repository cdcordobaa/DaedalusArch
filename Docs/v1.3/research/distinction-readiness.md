# Distinction readiness: the case for a Tesis Meritoria (or Laureada)

**Date:** 2026-10-16. **Branch:** `v1.3-distinction-readiness`, from `origin/v1.3` at `d65fddbb`. **Type:** research
and planning note. No code, result or sealed file was touched.

**Scope.** This is a master's thesis: MSc in Ingeniería de Sistemas y Computación, Facultad de Ingeniería, UNAL
Bogotá. The goal is a merit distinction for that thesis (Meritoria, or at most Laureada), not a doctoral contribution.
In this note, "doctoral rigour" means rigour of method inside master's scope. Every doctoral-scale idea is in §6
(Future Work) and is not claimed.

**How this note was verified.** Every regulation in §1 was read in its full official text, either on a UNAL server or
in a pre-captcha Wayback snapshot of the official legal database. Each quote gives its URL. Anything that could not
be read is marked **UNVERIFIED** and appears in the list of questions for the programme secretary (§1.6). No rule in
this note is inferred or invented.

## 0. The verdict in five lines

1. **There is a hard publication criterion, and it is the binding gap.**
   - Under the Facultad's rule (Acuerdo 104/2020 CF, Art. 30), the jurados may recommend a Meritoria only if the
     student shows at least one of these, as first author and crediting UNAL:
     - an **oral presentation at an international congress**;
     - an **indexed journal article that is accepted or published** (Publindex, JCR, SCI or SJR);
     - patent potential;
     - another MinCiencias "nuevo conocimiento" product.
   - Today the thesis has **none** of these. It has four drafts and no submission.
2. **A submitted indexed-journal article is a degree requirement for any research-track master's (Art. 32), with or
   without a distinction.** Paper 1 should therefore go to a journal (EMSE) now, whatever else happens.
3. **Methodological rigour is the thesis's strongest asset.** It has a registered Study 1, a replication package that
   reproduces 482 of 531 headline numbers with none overturned, explicit threats registers and tonight's non-human
   validity evidence. This fits criterion (b), "calidad excepcional de la argumentación", well.
4. **The weakest asset is the clarity of the single main contribution.** There are four papers, about 160 PRs, MCP,
   ontology, ISE, cheap proxies and a judge. A jury could read this as scope sprawl. The thesis needs one sentence
   that every chapter serves.
5. **The biggest avoidable risk is Study 2.** It runs once. If it is badly designed or hits the floor, it can hurt
   more than it helps. It must be framed in advance so that a null or floor result is still a reportable finding.

### 1.3 The Facultad de Ingeniería rule: Acuerdo 104 de 2020 Consejo de Facultad

Source: <http://web.archive.org/web/20230926170508/http://www.legal.unal.edu.co/rlunal/home/doc.jsp?d_i=97946>.
It was issued on 20 May 2020 and is applicable from 12 Apr 2021; Art. 53 derogates Acuerdo 040 de 2017. Under Art.
54, students who started earlier may opt in.

**Art. 27 (jury).** "el Consejo de Facultad, a petición del Comité Asesor de Posgrado, nombrará dos jurados para la
Tesis de Maestría. El director de la tesis podrá sugerir los jurados."

**Art. 28 par. 3 (defence).**
- "Exposición del estudiante: tendrá una duración máxima de 30 minutos."
- "Sesión de preguntas: intervendrán únicamente los jurados y el estudiante."
- Point 4: "En caso de resultar Aprobada, el jurado deliberará si considera que la tesis amerita alguna distinción:
  Meritoria o Laureada. Si hay unanimidad al respecto, se registrará en el acta la recomendación de distinción.
  Posteriormente, los jurados elaborarán una comunicación escrita dirigida al Consejo de Facultad de Ingeniería en
  donde se justifique la distinción recomendada."

**Art. 30 (Meritoria).** "el Consejo de Facultad puede otorgar distinción meritoria, la justificación de la propuesta
de distinción debe ser por escrito, motivada y unánime por parte del jurado, y debe ser presentada dentro de los
treinta (30) días calendario siguientes a partir de la fecha de la sustentación".

- **Par. 1 (what the jurados weigh):**
  - "a) Carácter sobresaliente de los resultados obtenidos en el contexto del tema de investigación en el ámbito
    nacional o internacional."
  - "b) Calidad excepcional de la argumentación científica, académica o técnica de los resultados de la tesis."
  - "c) Nivel de los eventos y revistas en que se han publicado o presentado resultados atribuibles a la investigación
    realizada, o potencialidad de obtención de patentes o registros de producto." The student must supply **at least
    one** of these:
    - "Al menos una ponencia oral del autor de la tesis en un congreso (evento académico) internacional en un tema
      atribuible a la Tesis de Maestría";
    - "Al menos, un artículo aceptado o publicado en una revista incluida en uno de los siguientes sistemas de
      indexación: Publindex, Journal Citation Reports (JCR), Science Citation Index (SCI) o SCimago Journal Rank (SJR),
      realizado durante el periodo en el cual se desarrolló la Tesis de Maestría";
    - patent, utility-model or industrial-design potential;
    - "Otros productos de nuevo conocimiento según la clasificación vigente en el Ministerio de Ciencia, Tecnología e
      Innovación".
- **Par. 2 (what the Consejo weighs, on the Comité de Directores de Área's recommendation):** "a) No exceder cinco
  (5) periodos de permanencia en el plan de estudios (incluyendo reservas de cupo)"; b) the Par. 1 criteria; c) the
  jurados' justification document.
- **Par. 3:** "el estudiante debe ser el primer autor."
- **Par. 4:** products must credit UNAL (Resolución 712 de 2015 de Rectoría).

**Art. 31 (Laureada).**
- The jurados request it "por escrito, motivada y unánime", within 30 calendar days.
- Par. 1: the jurados apply Art. 2 of Acuerdo 054/2012 CA.
- Par. 2: the Consejo de Facultad proposes it to the Consejo Académico if all of these hold: a) at most 5 periods;
  b) the Art. 30 par. 1 criteria; c) the justification; d) "Ser una contribución significativa al área de
  conocimiento correspondiente."

**Art. 32 (degree requirement, research-track master's).** This applies whether or not a distinction is sought.

> "Evidencia de un artículo sometido en una revista incluida en uno de los siguientes sistemas de indexación:
> Publindex, Journal Citation Reports (JCR), Science Citation Index (SCI) o SCimago Journal Rank (SJR), relacionado
> con la Tesis de Maestría, en el cual el estudiante sea el primer autor y que además dé crédito a la Universidad
> Nacional de Colombia."

### 2.2 What examiners look for (literature)

The citation metadata below was verified through Crossref and arXiv. The one-line readings are a summary of
well-known findings in each work. They are not quotations, and I did not re-read the full texts for this note.

- **Mullins & Kiley (2002)**, "'It's a PhD, not a Nobel Prize': how experienced examiners assess research theses",
  *Studies in Higher Education* 27(4):369–386, <https://doi.org/10.1080/0307507022000011507>. Experienced examiners
  form a view early, from the opening chapters, and reward coherence, a clear contribution and candour about
  limitations over sheer volume.
- **Bourke & Holbrook (2013)**, "Examining PhD and research masters theses", *Assessment & Evaluation in Higher
  Education* 38(4):407–416, <https://doi.org/10.1080/02602938.2011.638738>. This study includes research master's
  theses. Examiner comments concentrate on the contribution, the literature and the analysis, and less on volume.
- **Holbrook, Bourke, Lovat & Dally (2004)**, "Investigating PhD thesis examination reports", *International Journal of
  Educational Research* 41(2):98–120, <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijer.2005.04.008>.
- **Holbrook, Bourke, Lovat & Fairbairn (2008)**, "Consistency and inconsistency in PhD thesis examination",
  *Australian Journal of Education* 52(1):36–48, <https://doi.org/10.1177/000494410805200103>.
- **Ralph et al. (2020)**, "Empirical Standards for Software Engineering Research", arXiv:2010.03525,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03525>. These are the ACM SIGSOFT Empirical Standards
  (<https://github.com/acmsigsoft/EmpiricalStandards>, CC0), with method-specific checklists: Engineering Research
  (design science), Experiments, Repository Mining and Artifacts. A software-engineering jury can check a thesis
  against the "essential" items of the matching standards. It is cheap insurance to include a one-page compliance
  table in an appendix (§4, G6).
- **ACM Artifact Review and Badging**
  (<https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-and-badging-current>). **UNVERIFIED**: the page returned
  403. The badge levels (available, functional, reusable; results reproduced) are widely used in SE venues. Confirm
  the current names before citing them.

**What this means for a software-engineering master's thesis** (my synthesis, not a regulation):
- A built artefact plus a measured claim about it beats a broad survey.
- The jury will test whether the evaluation measures the artefact against something independent of its author (the
  circularity attack).
- The jury will check whether the claims stay within the data.
- The jury will check whether the objectives promised in the proposal were met or explicitly re-scoped.
