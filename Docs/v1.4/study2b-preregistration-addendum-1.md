# Study 2b pre-registration, addendum 1: deviations registered at the readiness gate

**Dated 2026-10-20T16:36:37Z (UTC), before any 2b call.** The pre-registration `Docs/v1.4/study2b-preregistration.md` is registered (2026-10-20T12:36:37Z) and its text is not changed: its sha256 stays the one in `Docs/v1.4/study2b-preregistration.registered.sha256`. Its own rule (header and §5, "Deviations after dating") is that any later change is a numbered, dated deviation that states what had been seen. This addendum is that record for the readiness gate (`Docs/v1.4/study2b-gate-verdict.md`). It goes with the OSF deposit, beside the pre-registration's hash.

**What had been seen when this was written.** No 2b output exists: no sealed task has been run, and no agent has opened a sealed file. The gate checks behind these deviations used 2a data (Stages A to C, on the 2a tasks), the dress rehearsal on a practice task, and three zero-call operations on the sealed set that print hashes and counts only: the `tasks.json` and `MANIFEST.sha256` hashes, the plan filler's counts, and the V11 leakage scan's counts of hashed 8-grams. No figure in this addendum comes from a sealed task's text or output.

**Who decided.** The coordinator, under GO-2B ("a red item is made green where that is realistic; if it cannot be, it is registered as a dated deviation before the run, with what is lost"), recorded as DELEG12 in `ops/decisions.md`. No never-waived item of GO-2B is touched.
