## 6. Aggregation and completeness (BR-U4-AGG, Q4 A, Q5 A)

| Id | Rule | Implements | Acceptance check |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-U4-AGG-01 | **Unit vote** over valid runs (`runsPerEvaluation = 3`): majority; a 1-1 split of 2 valid runs → `warning`; fewer than 2 valid runs → unit invalid (`INSUFFICIENT_VALID_RUNS` warning).
