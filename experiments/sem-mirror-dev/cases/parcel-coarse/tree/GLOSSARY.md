# Corner Shop orders

Customers order goods online; a courier brings the parcel. We tell the customer when the parcel is on its way and
when it has arrived. When the courier cannot hand it over, the customer gets an apology, and a parcel that comes back
to us is refunded.

A delivery is **pending** until the customer has the parcel (**delivered**) or it cannot reach them (**failed**). A
failed delivery is refunded.
