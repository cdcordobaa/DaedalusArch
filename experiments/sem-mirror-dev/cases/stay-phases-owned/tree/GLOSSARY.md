# Harbour Inn front desk

The front desk books rooms and charges guests. A stay moves through five phases; the inn's house rules attach to them:

- **reserved**: a room is promised but no deposit is paid. A reserved room is released 48 hours before arrival if the
  deposit has not come in.
- **guaranteed**: the deposit is paid. The room is kept until midnight of the arrival day; a guest who never arrives
  pays the first night.
- **in-house**: the guest holds a key. Room service and minibar charges post to the folio.
- **departed**: the folio is closed; nothing more may be charged.
- **cancelled**: the stay is off. A guaranteed stay cancelled inside 48 hours pays half a night.

The channel manager happens to track the same five steps under its own codes.
