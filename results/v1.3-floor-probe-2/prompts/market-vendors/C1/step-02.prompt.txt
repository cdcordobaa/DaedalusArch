Feature request:
Add vendor fees and payouts. Each pitch size has a stall fee per market day, with a surcharge for power, and the market keeps a commission of every card sale (5% by default; the organiser can set a different rate per vendor). A vendor can cancel a booking up to 48 hours before the market day without paying the stall fee. After a market day is closed, the organiser runs its settlement: each vendor's payout is their card sales for the day minus the commission and the stall fee. A payout is never negative: what the vendor still owes is carried into their next settlement. A settled market day can no longer receive sales or booking changes.

Keep the source under src/ and follow the architecture the project already uses (Clean Architecture).
