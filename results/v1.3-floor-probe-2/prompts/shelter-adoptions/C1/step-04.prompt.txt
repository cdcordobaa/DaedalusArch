Feature request:
The shelter needs an audit trail for its licence inspections. Every change to an animal, an application, a foster placement or a notification's delivery status is recorded with who made it (the staff member id from a request header, or 'system' for webhooks), when, and the values before and after. Staff can list the audit trail of one animal, including the changes to its applications and placements, and list every change one staff member made in a date range. A change and its audit record are always saved together or not at all.

Keep the source under src/ and follow the architecture the project already uses (Hexagonal Architecture, ports and adapters).
Organise the source code under src/ in exactly five folders: src/domain, src/application, src/adapters/inbound, src/adapters/outbound and src/config.
