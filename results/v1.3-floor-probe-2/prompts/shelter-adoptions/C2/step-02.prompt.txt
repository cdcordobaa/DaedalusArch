Feature request:
Add a foster network. Volunteers sign up as foster carers, with the number and the species of animals they can host. Staff place an animal with a carer and end the placement. A carer never hosts more animals than their capacity. An animal in foster care can still be adopted: approving its adoption ends the placement and e-mails the carer.

Keep the source under src/ and follow the architecture the project already uses (Hexagonal Architecture, ports and adapters).
