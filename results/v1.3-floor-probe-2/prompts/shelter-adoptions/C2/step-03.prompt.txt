Feature request:
Applicants and carers can now choose to be notified by SMS instead of e-mail. SMS messages go through TextBridge: its HTTP API takes a phone number and a text of at most 160 characters, and it reports each message's delivery later, by calling a webhook of ours. When TextBridge reports a failed delivery, the same notification is sent by e-mail instead. Staff can see the delivery status of every notification.

Keep the source under src/ and follow the architecture the project already uses (Hexagonal Architecture, ports and adapters).
