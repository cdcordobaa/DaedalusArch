Whether a rider may join an open pool must now be judged on RouteKit's road times instead of our straight-line estimate. The RouteKit SDK, the package routekit-sdk, is already in the project and the client is already created with the key in ROUTEKIT_API_KEY for the pickup-area check; use the same client and ask it for car routes. A pool's time is RouteKit's road time through the pool's stops in their order. With the rider, the rider's pickup and then the rider's drop-off come just before the pool's last stop, as today. The added time is the time with the rider minus the time without, in seconds. A rider may join a pool only when the added time is at most 8 minutes; exactly 8 minutes is allowed. Among the pools that allow it, the rider joins the one with the smallest added time, and on equal added times the pool opened first. Full pools are not considered and RouteKit is not asked about them; when no open pool has a free seat, RouteKit is not asked at all. With no pool that allows it, a new pool is opened for the rider. The answer still gives the added time rounded to the nearest whole minute, and 0 for a new pool. Our own checks on the rider's name, the coordinates and the pickup area still run first and still refuse a bad request as before, without asking RouteKit for routes.
