Change request:
Patients can reply STOP to a reminder. Textwave then refuses every text to that number with its blocked-recipient error, giving the reason that the recipient opted out. From the first such refusal on, the clinic must remember that the number opted out and never try to text it again, neither reminders nor booking confirmations: no attempt at all. A reminder refused or skipped for this reason is 'opted out' in the run's answer and is not retried. On the front desk list, a reminder that was not sent because the number opted out shows 'opted out' with callPatient: true; a reminder that was sent before the opt-out keeps showing Textwave's report. A booking for a number that opted out is still booked and answers confirmation: 'opted out' instead of 'sent'; a booking whose confirmation Textwave refuses because the recipient opted out answers the same and the number is remembered. Textwave also refuses numbers that the phone carrier blocks, with the same error and the reason carrier blocked. That is not an opt-out: the number is not remembered and is tried again next time. Such a reminder is 'not sent' (callPatient: true), is not retried in the same run, and the run goes on; a booking whose confirmation is refused this way is still booked and answers confirmation: 'not sent'. An opt-out refusal decides even on a try after throttling. A number is the same number whatever spaces were typed in it.
