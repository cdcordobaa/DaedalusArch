Change request:
When our own fridge cannot cover a ward request, the missing units must now be ordered from Haemalink, the regional blood supplier. The Haemalink SDK, the package haemalink-sdk, is already in the project and the client is already created with the key in HAEMALINK_API_KEY and our site code in HAEMALINK_SITE for following deliveries; use the same client. Our own checks on the ward, the component, the blood group and the number of units still run first and still refuse a bad request as before, without taking any unit and without asking Haemalink. Our fridge is still used first. Only the units it cannot give are ordered, in one Haemalink order with one line for the requested component and blood group; when the fridge covers the whole request, Haemalink is not asked. Urgent requests are ordered with Haemalink's emergency priority, all others with its routine priority. Haemalink may confirm fewer units than ordered, or none. The answer keeps fromStock, outstanding and status, and adds fromSupplier, the units Haemalink confirmed, and supplierOrder, Haemalink's order number, or null when Haemalink was not asked. outstanding is what neither gave. status is 'filled' when nothing is outstanding, 'unfilled' when neither the fridge nor Haemalink gave a unit, and 'partly filled' otherwise. If Haemalink fails to answer, the request fails and takes none of our stock.
