Change request:
Urgent red cell requests must be covered with O negative red cells when their own blood group cannot be given. For an urgent red cell request, the units that neither our fridge nor Haemalink gives in the requested group are taken next from our own O negative red cells, and whatever is still missing is ordered from Haemalink as O negative red cells with emergency priority, in a second order placed after the first. The answer adds substituted, the number of O negative units given in place of the requested group, from the fridge and from Haemalink together, and coverOrder, the number of the O negative order, or null when none was placed; supplierOrder stays the number of the first order. fromStock and fromSupplier count only units of the requested group, and outstanding is what is still missing after the cover. There is no cover for routine requests, for platelets or plasma, or for requests that are already O negative; for those substituted is 0 and coverOrder is null. When our O negative fridge covers what is missing, Haemalink is not asked for O negative. A request whose own group is on hold is still covered with O negative, without asking Haemalink for its own group, and its holdReason and holdUntil still tell the hold of its own group. O negative red cells are put on hold and kept on hold exactly as any other group: while they are on hold Haemalink is not asked for them, and those units stay outstanding. holdReason, holdUntil and the 'on hold' status only ever tell the hold of the requested group, never an O negative hold met while covering. For the status, substituted units count as given. If the O negative order fails in any way other than an embargo, the whole request fails and takes none of our stock.
