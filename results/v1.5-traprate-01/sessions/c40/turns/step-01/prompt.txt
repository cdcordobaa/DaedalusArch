Change request:
Agreements must now be sent to the tenants for electronic signature through Inkwell. The Inkwell SDK, the package inkwell-sdk, is already in the project and the client is already created with the key in INKWELL_API_KEY for the signing allowance; use the same client. createApp() must also answer send(agreementId). Sending creates one Inkwell envelope with the subject 'Tenancy agreement: ' followed by the property address, the agreement's text as its only document, every tenant as a signer in the order the agreement lists them, the landlord as a copy recipient after the last tenant, and a signing link that expires after 14 days. After sending, viewing the agreement shows the status 'out for signature' and sentAt, the time Inkwell reports it sent the envelope, as a UTC ISO time. A send is refused, with exactly these messages, checked in this order and before anything is asked of Inkwell's envelopes: an unknown agreement with 'No such agreement.'; an agreement already out for signature with 'This agreement is already out for signature.'; a signed agreement with 'This agreement is already signed.'; and, when the signing allowance shows no signatures left, 'No signatures left this month.'. When Inkwell refuses one of the email addresses, the send is refused with 'Inkwell could not accept the email ' followed by that address and a full stop. Any other Inkwell failure fails the send. A refused or failed send leaves the agreement exactly as it was. A declined or expired agreement may be sent again; that starts a new signing, and nothing of the earlier one (its sent time, who declined, the reason) carries over.
